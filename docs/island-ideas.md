# The island as a place for stats: ideas, not built

The owner, 2026-09-19: the island is not for saying how an agent is doing. It is a medium for
showing what Builda already knows, in a way no other screen does. It expands and blows up, and it
is not up all the time. Nothing here is built. Pick one or none.

Every idea uses numbers the app already has (the profile graph, records, the Wrapped cards) and
appears only when a person asks (a tap or a pull) or when something is news (a record).

1. **Pull the week out of the notch.** Pull down from the top edge and this week's seven days drop
   out of the island as pixel columns, tallest day last. Let go and they fold back in.
2. **One number, rolled.** Tap the island area inside the app and it widens just enough for one
   figure: today's hours, rolling up digit by digit. A second tap moves to the next figure
   (streak, top project, longest sitting).
3. **Record, once.** When a sitting sets a personal record, the island grows into the record
   itself (the number and what it beat) for one beat, then leaves. Only on that day.
4. **This week against last.** The island splits in two, left half last week and right half this
   week, and the line between them slides to show which is ahead.
5. **The heatmap unrolls.** Long press and the month's heatmap unrolls downward out of the island
   like a scroll, a row of days at a time, then rolls back up.
6. **Monday's headline.** The first open on a Monday, the island expands into last week's one
   headline number, and a tap opens last week's card.
7. **The creature holds it.** Your creature peeks out of the notch holding a sign with one stat,
   and ducks back in.

What would decide between them: which numbers people actually open (the app already knows which
cards get shared), and whether it should happen by itself at all. 3 and 6 happen by themselves;
the rest wait for a hand.
